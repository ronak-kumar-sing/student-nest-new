# 🚀 StudentNest App - Quick Installation Guide

## 📱 Installation Instructions

### For Android Users

#### Method 1: Direct APK Installation (Recommended)
1. **Download the APK** from the link provided to you
2. **Enable Unknown Sources:**
   - Go to Settings → Security
   - Enable "Unknown Sources" or "Install Unknown Apps"
   - Select your browser and allow installation
3. **Install the APK:**
   - Open the downloaded APK file
   - Tap "Install"
   - Wait for installation to complete
4. **Open StudentNest:**
   - Tap "Open" after installation
   - Or find the StudentNest icon in your app drawer

#### Method 2: Google Play Store (Coming Soon)
- Search for "StudentNest" on Google Play Store
- Tap "Install"

## ✨ First Time Setup

1. **Grant Permissions:**
   - Location: To find nearby accommodations
   - Camera: To upload property photos (for owners)
   - Storage: To save images

2. **Create Account:**
   - Sign up with email or phone number
   - Verify your account
   - Complete your profile

3. **Start Exploring:**
   - Search for rooms near your college
   - Filter by price, amenities, and distance
   - Book your perfect student accommodation!

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Issues

**App won't install?**
- Make sure you enabled "Unknown Sources"
- Check if you have enough storage space
- Try redownloading the APK

**Can't log in?**
- Check your internet connection
- Verify your email/phone number
- Try "Forgot Password" option

**Location not working?**
- Grant location permissions
- Enable GPS on your device
- Restart the app
